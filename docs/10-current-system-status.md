# 10 — Current kOA System Status and Limits

## 1. Why this document exists

The credibility of this dossier depends on separating **architectural maturity**, **implemented components**, **publicly active workflows**, and **production deployment**.

The whole kOA ecosystem should not be presented as production-complete.

## 2. Public status as of 2026-08-17

### kOA public hub
https://initkoa.org/

The current public framing describes kOA as a system of systems for governable knowledge-to-action, with Konnaxion, Orgo, Kristal, and related components.

## 3. What can be claimed safely

The evidence supports saying that kOA is:

- a substantial, multi-repository sociotechnical architecture;
- documented through public repositories, wikis, technical specifications, schemas, and context packs;
- implemented to varying degrees across Konnaxion, Orgo, Kristal, SemantiK, kOA-Linux, and related systems;
- actively developed and increasingly integrated;
- already explicit about authority, provenance, lifecycle, portability, institutional memory, and capability-related workflows.

## 4. What should not be claimed without additional proof

Avoid saying that:

- every module is production-complete;
- every documented invariant is fully enforced in deployed code;
- the complete ecosystem has already been validated at institutional scale;
- the architecture empirically proves its social theory;
- all concepts were first formulated two years ago unless timestamped evidence is assembled.

## 5. Why the limitation strengthens the research proposal

The research value does not depend on claiming a finished universal platform. It depends on a more defensible proposition:

> Building a sufficiently complex, partially implemented sociotechnical system forced a set of distinctions to become explicit. Those distinctions now generate hypotheses that can be tested in a domain independent of the architecture.
